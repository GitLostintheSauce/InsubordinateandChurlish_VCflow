Where the Money Flowed: VC Funding 2022–2025

Annual venture funding across six sectors, plus the crypto cycle quarter-by-quarter.

The short version: AI went from one sector among many to roughly half of all venture capital by 2025 ($45.8B → $211B). Crypto crashed in 2022–23 and is rebuilding. Fintech halved, then recovered. The money concentrated — into AI, and into fewer, bigger rounds.
