🤖 AI — the sector that ate venture

The defining shift of 2022–2025, and where its appetite is now pulling capital next.

AI's takeover of VC

No sector reshaped venture capital like AI. Funding climbed from $45.8B in 2022 to $211B in 2025 — by which point AI was roughly half of all global venture dollars. And it concentrated: per CB Insights' Venture Trends 2025, about 49% of AI funding went to just six companies, and a single OpenAI round (~$40B) was ~19% of the year's AI total. (Charts 1 & 2 above show the full trajectory and the swelling share of the pie.)

The AI–energy nexus

The question: Aligned — one of the six top-funded AI companies — builds AI data centers, which are enormously power-hungry. So: is venture money following AI's power demand into energy? Is it correlated? Do startups or incumbents capture it? And how did it shift from 2024 → 2025?
Did energy VC rise?
Yes — sharply. Next-gen nuclear VC hit ~$2.4B in 2024 (~12× the prior year); fusion ~$2.64B/yr. Rounds scaled into the billions in 2025 — X-energy $1.4B, Base Power $1B, Commonwealth Fusion $863M.
Why?
AI data centers are power-hungry. US data-center electricity went from 4.4% of the grid (2023) toward 7–12% by 2028. Capital follows the demand — see the driver chart below.
Startups or incumbents?
Both — in different forms. Startups raise the VC (measurable, billions). Incumbents lock up the actual gigawatts via hyperscaler PPAs (Amazon–Talen 1.92 GW, Meta–Constellation 1.12 GW) at undisclosed prices — a bigger, unmeasurable channel.

⚠ The honest caveat — read before quoting a number

"Energy for AI" isn't a tracked funding category, so any precise 2024→2025 total or multiple is unreliable — the bars are selected named rounds that under-count the true sector totals (broader aggregates put next-gen nuclear + fusion VC at ~$2.4B in 2024 alone). On 2024 fission specifically: startups raised ~$0.57B in the named VC rounds shown (X-energy $500M Oct 2024, Aalo, Last Energy) plus a separate $303M DOE grant — and corporate-backed nuclear VC was ~$645M in 2024 (Global Venturing). So fission 2024 was mostly VC, not grants. What is solid: the direction (sharply up) and the driver (AI power demand) — not a clean multiple. VC and government grants are shown separately; non-VC money (Oklo's SPAC, Intersect's infrastructure PE, Crusoe's vertically-integrated model) is excluded. Correlation, not proven causation.
