03The concentration event

Capital didn't rotate into AI — it concentrated. Four cuts of the 145 landmark deals show that the megaround era was a handful of giant, repeat rounds in a few AI labs, while Web3 stayed a crowd. This is the spine of the dashboard; the sections below are evidence and context.

1 · The megaround era

Every landmark round by date and size (log scale). The average round ballooned from ~$440M (2022) to ~$2.6B (2025). Hover any dot for the deal.

2 · Capital magnets

Companies that raised more than one landmark round, sized by total capital pulled in. Three AI labs — OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI — absorbed ~$93B, nearly half of all $197B tracked here.

3 · A king vs a crowd

Share of each sector's landmark capital held by its single biggest round, its next two, and everyone else. AI and Defense are winner-take-most; Web3 is a long tail.

4 · The capital map: US vs rest of world

Where the landmark capital actually sits. AI and Defense are almost entirely American; Climate is the outlier — roughly 70% non-US, driven by European battery gigafactories.

Shares are of landmark-deal capital (145 cited rounds), not whole-market totals — a curated sample, so read it as direction, not census. Country is HQ as recorded in landmark_deals.csv.

07The downstream play: AI-energy

The concentration into a few AI labs has a second-order consequence: someone has to power them. Below — selected named rounds and the demand curve pulling the capital.

Selected AI-energy rounds

Illustrative named rounds by subsector and capital class. Under-counts the true market — read as direction, not census.

Data-center electricity demand

The driver behind the funding question: US data-center demand is projected to roughly double or triple by 2028.

Methodology + caveat — read before quoting

"Energy for AI" is not a clean tracked category. The bars show selected named rounds and under-count the true market. The stronger claim is directional: AI power demand is pulling capital toward nuclear, fusion, geothermal, grid hardware, and storage, alongside large undisclosed hyperscaler PPAs. Correlation is not proof of causation.
