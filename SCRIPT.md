# Pitch script — VC Flow dashboard

Speaker notes for presenting the dashboard to a **perp DEX founder (GTE)**.
Every figure below is already on the page and sourced in [`sources.md`](sources.md) — no number here is invented.

## The through-line (say this if you say nothing else)

> Capital concentrates in a few winners. Right now it's rotating *back into crypto trading infrastructure* — the strongest Web3 quarter since early 2022, and into rails, not consumer froth. **That is exactly GTE's category, and these markets fund the category leader.**

The dashboard reads top-to-bottom as a build: set up the macro, then land on the part of the market that matters for GTE. Treat **§06 (Web3 cycle) as the climax** — slow down there. §07 (AI-energy) and §08 (Compare) are supporting material; don't let them dilute the §06 peak.

## Section-by-section walk

### §03 — The concentration event *(open here, not at the top of the page)*
> "Start with what everyone already feels: AI ate venture capital. Of AI's **$211B** in 2025 funding, **~$93B — nearly half of all $197B in tracked landmark capital — went to just three labs**: OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI. AI took **~50% of all global venture dollars** in 2025, up from one category among several three years ago. Hold one idea: *capital concentrates.*"

### §02 — Headline insights *(rapid evidence — let them scan, don't read aloud)*
> "These ten cards are the whole story in numbers — AI, Web3, fintech — and every one is sourced."

### §05 — Macro context *(the bridge — set up the contrast)*
> "Here's the full field off one consistent Crunchbase backbone. AI's curve dwarfs everything in absolute dollars. But absolute size isn't the opportunity — the opportunity is where capital is *moving*. So look at what's recovering underneath the AI story."

### §06 — The Web3 cycle *(STOP. This is the pitch.)*
> "This is the chart that matters for GTE. Web3 quarterly funding collapsed **86%** — from **$14.2B in Q1 2022 to $1.93B in Q4 2023** — the winter. It's now back at **$8.5B in Q4 2025, the strongest quarter since Q2 2022.** And read *where* it went: **infrastructure and trading rails, not the consumer/NFT froth of 2022.** That's my category. The money didn't just come back to crypto — it came back to *what GTE builds.*"
>
> *(Tie back to §03:)* "And concentration isn't only an AI story — a single **$2B Binance/MGX deal was ~41% of all Web3 funding in Q1 2025.** These markets fund the category leader. GTE is positioned to be that leader in perp DEX."

### §04 — The 145-deal leaderboard *(credibility, on demand — great for Q&A)*
> "If anyone doubts a number, it's all here: 145 deals, every row sourced and filterable. Filter to Web3 and you can see the comparable rounds yourself."

### §07 — AI-energy *(acknowledge, move past — one line)*
> "There's a downstream AI-energy story too, but that's not our lane — I'll skip it unless you want to dig in."

### §08 — Sector comparison *(hand them the controls — strong interactive close)*
> "Last thing — compare any sectors yourself, each rebased to its 2022 level so relative growth is visible regardless of size. Put Web3 against fintech or AI. The relative-growth story is the one I've been telling you."

## Supporting numbers you can drop in if pressed

- **Megaround inflation:** average landmark round grew **~6×**, from ~$440M (2022) to ~$2.6B (2025).
- **OpenAI's scale:** its single Q1 2025 round was **~19%** of all AI funding for the year.
- **Fintech as a discipline analogy:** halved from **$90.2B (2022) to $43B (2023)**, then rebuilt to **$51.8B (2025)** on fewer, larger rounds — a recovery shaped like Web3's.

## Anticipated questions

- *"Isn't this just the crypto hype cycle again?"* — No: the §06 data shows the money returned to **infrastructure and trading rails**, a structurally different mix than 2021–22's consumer/NFT peak.
- *"Why should capital pick GTE?"* — Concentration. In every sector shown, a small number of names absorb most of the capital (§03 AI; §06 the 41% Web3 deal). The pitch is that GTE is the leader candidate in perp DEX.
- *"Where do these numbers come from?"* — Crunchbase annual backbone for cross-sector comparison; Galaxy Digital quarterly for the Web3 deep dive; all traced in `sources.md` (§01 Methodology).
